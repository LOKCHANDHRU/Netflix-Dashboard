# Netflix-Dashboard

# project objective
This project aims to analyze Netflix’s global content library through an interactive Power BI dashboard, highlighting trends in movies and TV shows across countries and genres. It provides insights into content distribution, release year patterns, and ratings to support data-driven decisions. The dashboard enhances understanding of viewer preferences and Netflix’s content strategy.

# Questions (KPIs)

 - Total Titles – Total number of movies and TV shows available on Netflix.
 - Total Movies – Count of all movies in the dataset.
 - Total TV Shows – Count of all TV shows in the dataset.
 - Movies vs TV Shows Ratio – Percentage distribution between movies and TV shows.
 - Total Countries Covered – Number of countries represented in the Netflix content library.
 - Total Directors – Count of unique directors across all titles.
 - Total Genres – Number of distinct genres available on the platform.
 - Most Common Genre – Genre with the highest number of titles.
 - Most Active Country – Country with the largest number of titles.
 - Content Added per Year – Number of titles added each year (trend analysis).
 - Average IMDb Rating (if available) – Average rating across all titles.
 - Top-Rated Titles – Highest-rated movies and TV shows.
 - Oldest & Newest Content Year – Range of release years in the dataset.
 - Most Featured Actor/Actress – Actor appearing in the most titles.
 - Language Distribution – Count or percentage of content by primary language.
 - Content Added by Month/Year – Trend of content additions over time.
 - Top 5 Countries by Content Count – Bar chart KPI to show country-wise dominance.
 - Average Duration (Movies) – Mean runtime of movies.
 - Average Seasons (TV Shows) – Mean number of seasons in TV shows.
 - Content Rating Distribution – Breakdown of age ratings (e.g., TV-MA, PG-13, etc.).
